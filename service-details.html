We Provide Civil Services

At Al Fawz, we believe that safety and sustainability go hand in hand. That is why we established a dedicated Safety Department committed to applying the highest standards of prevention and protection across all our projects. This department plays a vital role in promoting awareness, building a strong safety culture among employees, and ensuring that every operation complies with approved procedures and international best practices.

For us, safety is not just a mandatory requirement — it is a core value deeply rooted in everything we do. We view a safe work environment as the foundation of successful project delivery, protecting not only our people and resources but also the communities we serve. By prioritizing safety, we ensure that our projects are executed efficiently, sustainably, and with a level of excellence that reflects our commitment to both clients and society.
